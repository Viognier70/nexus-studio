## Purpose

Traditions create continuity, belonging and professional identity. They are not cosmetic seasonal events.

## Initial tradition set

- debutant cocktail,
- Mårten Goose dinner,
- winter ball,
- Christmas celebration,
- spring ball,
- practical and oral examinations,
- church-based graduation ceremony,
- graduation celebration with family and friends.

## Craft traditions

- flambé service,
- carving and portioning,
- table setting,
- napkin folding,
- service utensils,
- professional French terminology,
- cigar knowledge as historical curriculum content,
- food and beverage pairing,
- etiquette,
- leadership,
- staging and atmosphere.

## Design rule

Historical traditions may be represented critically and contextually. Nexus should preserve knowledge without uncritically reproducing outdated norms.

## Game functions

Traditions can provide:

- competitions,
- examinations,
- reputation moments,
- social encounters,
- temporary employment,
- mentorship,
- rare business opportunities,
- collective memories.

## Ceremony principle

Important accomplishments should sometimes be recognised through a shared ceremony rather than a pop-up badge.

## MVP use

The first prototype includes one simplified welcome ceremony and one seasonal practical assessment.
