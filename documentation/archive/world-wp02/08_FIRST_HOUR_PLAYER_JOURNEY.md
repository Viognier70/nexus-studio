**Owner:** UX and Game Design

## 0–5 minutes: Arrival

The player arrives, observes and walks toward registration.

## 5–12 minutes: Human encounter

The player meets an applicant, alumnus or staff member. One short dialogue establishes that many different backgrounds are welcome.

## 12–25 minutes: Initiation sample

The player completes:

- one factual question,
- one practical ordering task,
- one ambiguous scenario,
- one reflection prompt.

No scores are shown yet.

## 25–35 minutes: First profile reveal

The system presents observations rather than labels:

- demonstrated knowledge,
- emerging strengths,
- one area for development,
- three available starting opportunities.

## 35–47 minutes: First meaningful choice

The player selects one opportunity, such as:

- assist at a welcome dinner,
- help a local café,
- join a research observation,
- prepare for a service task.

## 47–57 minutes: Consequence

The chosen task produces a visible consequence affecting a person, event or organisation.

## 57–60 minutes: Return hook

Before leaving, the player sees:

- a future event,
- one person worth following,
- one locked opportunity with a clear reason,
- a small change in Grythyttan caused by the player's action.

## Completion criteria

The player has not yet opened a company. The first hour establishes identity, agency, social connection and future possibility.
