## Principle

NPCs must represent institutions and local life without replacing player-to-player relationships.

## Initial groups

### Applicants and students

Newcomers with varied backgrounds, dreams and competence.

### Faculty and craft teachers

Represent theory, practice, standards and institutional memory.

### Researchers

Generate questions, studies, observations and debates.

### Local residents

Provide continuity, local knowledge, needs and resistance to change.

### Business owners

Employ, compete, mentor, invest and sometimes fail.

### Municipality

Controls selected premises, permits, local development and public-interest scenarios.

### Bank

Assesses financing, market understanding, risk and trust.

### Suppliers and producers

Provide ingredients, beverages, equipment, credit and supply-chain scenarios.

### Alumni and respected actors

Offer mentorship, recruitment, consulting and investment.

## Player substitution rule

As the player population grows, suitable player roles should gradually replace NPC functions where this improves social interaction without making essential services unavailable.
