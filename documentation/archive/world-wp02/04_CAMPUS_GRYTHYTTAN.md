## Purpose

Campus is the institutional heart of The Origin. It connects learning, craft, research, culture and professional recognition.

## Core functions

- initiation and screening,
- knowledge modules,
- practical simulations,
- research roles,
- mentorship,
- public lectures,
- examinations,
- ceremonies,
- recruitment events.

## Player states supported

- applicant,
- student,
- visiting professional,
- researcher,
- doctoral candidate,
- mentor,
- examiner,
- alumnus/alumna,
- investor or employer attending an event.

## Cultural principle

Campus must not feel like a menu of courses. It should feel like an institution with memory, expectations and rituals.

## Key spaces

- registration courtyard,
- practical dining room,
- sensory laboratory,
- seminar room,
- research archive,
- ceremonial hall,
- alumni room,
- campus noticeboard.

## Interaction design

Players should encounter knowledge through situated prompts:

- preparing a table before a dinner,
- observing service,
- discussing a research finding,
- responding to a leadership dilemma,
- making a practical judgement under time pressure.

## Long-term role

Experienced players may return to campus to:

- mentor,
- teach,
- examine,
- recruit,
- fund research,
- propose scenarios,
- participate in annual ceremonies.
