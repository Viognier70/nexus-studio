## Purpose

Define the player's first five minutes in Nexus.

## Emotional target

The arrival should create anticipation without spectacle. Grythyttan must feel small, real and unexpectedly significant.

## Sequence

### 00:00–01:30 — The road

The player sits on a regional bus travelling through forest and lake country. Interaction is limited to looking, observing and selecting one object to inspect:

- admission folder,
- neighbouring passenger,
- landscape,
- luggage tag.

The selection does not assign a role. It records an initial curiosity signal.

### 01:30–02:20 — The missed place

The village is visually easy to pass. A road sign appears briefly. The sequence should convey the paradox that a small place can contain a large intellectual and cultural ambition.

### 02:20–03:30 — Arrival

The bus stops. Doors open. Ambient sound replaces the bus interior. The player steps onto gravel with other applicants.

### 03:30–05:00 — First sight

The Sevilla Pavilion becomes visible. The player regains full movement and may walk toward campus, remain at the stop or speak to another applicant.

## First line of dialogue

A fellow applicant may say:

> “I nearly missed the village. Perhaps that is the point.”

## UX requirements

- No minimap during the first three minutes.
- No currency, level or statistics are shown.
- One subtle objective appears only after the player has looked around: `Find the registration table`.
- Mobile version may compress the bus sequence to 45–60 seconds while preserving agency.

## Acceptance criteria

- The player can identify Grythyttan as a real place.
- The player experiences at least one voluntary observation.
- The sequence does not resemble a conventional tutorial.
