## Candidate identifications (unverified)

- The three polygons cluster near where the Sörälgsvägen roundabout enters the campus (visible in the 2008 aerial 2 above). They may be:
  - **Entrance kiosk / gate lodge** — small structure controlling access, visible as a dark low building in the 2008 aerial.
  - **Kitchen-garden utility building** — Köksträdgården (designed by landscape architect Simon Irving, early 2000s) sits between Måltidens hus and Lake Sörälgen; a small garden shed / greenhouse would be typical.
  - **Grounds-maintenance building** — small tool shed for the campus lawns.

None of these identifications is verifiable from public sources.

## Buildings NOT located in this zone (per Akademiska Hus records)

- **Hälsans Hus (T0014003)** is at **Carl Jans väg**, not Sörälgsvägen. It is currently vacant with plans to be dismantled and moved (per Nerikes Allehanda 2025-08-23). It is **not** one of `w611766155/6/7`.
- **Kärnhuset (T0014002)** is at Sörälgsvägen 1, ~160 m WEST of Måltidens hus. Its own manifest lives in `../karnhuset/`.

## Recommendation

Do NOT model these three polygons in District 2 PHASE 1. They should render at generic OsmBuildings fidelity (the shed / outbuilding sub-typology introduced in ORDER 011 will treat them as small sheds, which is defensible for buildings of 55–128 m² footprint).

If the Vision Owner later identifies any of them (e.g. as a specific garden shed or entrance kiosk), a targeted follow-up can promote just that polygon.
