## 1. Why this order exists

The Vision Owner played the built cycle on 2026-08-10 and found the stream saying things like:

> *Ett bord fick bestick utan smörknivar.*

His verdict:

> The side panel describing what happens is relatively meaningless. *"A butter knife was missing"* — what does that mean? Well, **that is the interesting part**: it is the consequence of having taken on cheaper staff, or staff who do not care or are not engaged enough — you did not invest in your people. The information has to give something.

The line reports a symptom and hides the cause. **This is the same error as the abstract sustainability stake, in a new form.**

And the Vision Owner named the physical chain the game is missing:

> An efficient service means the staff can work in rhythm. If mise en place is not done properly it creates a great deal of running about and unnecessary load on the staff — ice that has run out, napkins that never got folded.

That chain — *morning preparation → rhythm or its absence → what the guest sees* — does not exist in Nexus. Without it, the stream can only report events; it cannot report consequences.

---

## 2. Mise en place

**Prep stops being a countdown and becomes work that either got done or did not.**

The prep window already exists (`ORDER 045` §7: staff work at ×1.8 pace during it). What it lacks is an outcome. Give it one: at the moment the doors open, the room has a **preparation state** — how much of what needed doing got done.

It is set by what the player decided and what the team could carry:
- **Team size and competence** — a thin team cannot prepare a full room in the same window.
- **Prep length** — the player's own choice.
- **Morning changes** — a menu rewritten that morning eats the prep window. This is the improvised-menu case the Vision Owner named.
- **What the business is carrying** — a longer wine list, a bigger room, more covers expected.

Report the model before building. Prep should be **specific, not a single score**: ice, napkins, cutlery, stations, garnish — a handful of concrete things, each done or not, so the shortfall has a name when it surfaces later.

---

## 3. Rhythm, and the pulse

**A service where mise en place was done runs in rhythm. One where it was not produces running about** — staff crossing the room for what should have been at hand, load that is nobody's fault and everybody's problem.

Show it. A **pulse reading, green through red**, visible during service: is the room working in time, or is it chasing itself?

This is not a new instrument in the `ORDER 047` §2 sense — it is the room's physical state, and it should read as the room, not as a dial. Report the form before building. A diagram, a pulse, a colour on the staff pucks themselves — the Vision Owner named diagram or pulse; pick and justify.

**Constraint:** per `EXECUTIVE_DESIGN_DIRECTIVE_001.md` §11 it cannot dominate, and per `ORDER 050` Addendum A §6.5 the room stays the protagonist. If the pulse can live *in* the room rather than beside it, that is better.

---

## 4. Every stream line carries its cause

**A line that reports a symptom without its cause is not worth the space it takes.**

> *En gäst frågade efter en smörkniv som saknades — mise en place blev kort efter det improviserade menybytet, och det märks på bordet.*

Rewrite the sentence banks so that lines arising from a traceable condition **name that condition**. Not all lines have one — ambient texture is still ambient — but any line that is a consequence must say what of.

The causes available: thin team, low competence, poor morale, short prep, a morning change, an ingredient tier, a scale-down still active, a supplier that short-delivered (`ORDER 051`).

**This does not mean explaining the mechanics.** It means the sentence says what happened *and why*, the way a professional would describe it to a colleague. `ORDER 048` §2's plain register still holds: short clauses, no interpretation, no observer voice during service.

## 5. The stream panel

Two faults from the playthrough:

- **It is sized for eight entries and holds one.** A large empty rectangle occupying a fifth of the screen. It should be the size of its contents.
- **It moves both ways.** Entries arrive at the bottom and the block grows upward, so the reading position shifts unpredictably. Pick one direction and hold it. Report which.

## 6. After the countdown, say what to do

The opening image ends, the doors open, and **nothing tells the player what happens now.**

The countdown is well built — weather, world factor, waiting guests, a clear number. Then it vanishes and the player is alone with a room.

Say what the service asks of them. One line, in the plain register, at the moment the doors open.

## 11. What this order is for

`ORDER 048` split the voice so the service could report plainly. It reports plainly and says nothing.

A missing butter knife is not an event. It is the last visible link in a chain that starts with a hiring decision and runs through a prep window that was too short. **This order builds the chain, and makes the sentence say it.**
