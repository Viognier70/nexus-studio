## PURPOSE

The physical reconstruction of Grythyttan is approaching completion.
From this point forward, the project shifts from primarily building a digital twin toward building a living educational simulation.
This document defines the long-term design principles for Nexus Studio.
It supersedes previous fragmented gameplay ideas.
All future gameplay design shall conform to this document.

## WHAT IS NEXUS STUDIO?

Nexus Studio is **not**:

- a quiz game
- a city builder
- a restaurant simulator
- an RPG
- a business game

It is all of these only insofar as they support a larger purpose.
Nexus Studio is:
**A living digital twin where professional competence develops through authentic action.**
The village is real.
The institutions are real.
The restaurants are real.
The knowledge is real.
The player's development is real.

## THE PLAYER

The player is not collecting points.
The player is developing professional competence.
The player becomes

- entrepreneur
- restaurateur
- leader
- innovator
- researcher
- citizen

within the living ecosystem of Grythyttan.

## THE WORLD

The digital twin is the foundation.
The world contains:

- buildings
- roads
- businesses
- restaurants
- producers
- forests
- lakes
- campus
- visitors
- residents
- NPCs

Everything else emerges from interactions.
The world itself is the engine.

## CORE GAME LOOP

The entire game revolves around one repeating cycle.
Explore
↓
Observe
↓
Encounter opportunities or problems
↓
Seek knowledge
↓
Acquire competence
↓
Invest competence
↓
Transform your restaurant, organisation or community
↓
The village changes
↓
New opportunities emerge
↓
More complex problems appear
↓
Repeat

## KNOWLEDGE IS THE PRIMARY RESOURCE

Knowledge is not content.
Knowledge is capital.
Knowledge creates opportunities.
Knowledge unlocks possibilities.
Knowledge changes the simulation.

## THREE KNOWLEDGE FORMS

Every activity contributes to one or more Aristotelian knowledge forms.

### Episteme

Knowing.
Scientific understanding.
Theory.
Research.
Facts.
Analysis.

### Techne

Doing.
Craftsmanship.
Methods.
Execution.
Production.
Skills.

### Phronesis

Judgement.
Wisdom.
Leadership.
Ethics.
Context.
Professional decision making.
Phronesis represents the highest level of professional competence.

## COMPETENCE IS BUILT THROUGH ACTION

The portfolio is never manually completed.
Everything is inferred from player behaviour.
Examples:
Repeated ethical decisions
↓
Phronesis evidence
Repeated successful menu optimisation
↓
Techne evidence
Repeated correct interpretation of research
↓
Episteme evidence
The player never earns competence.
The player demonstrates competence.

## THE PROFESSIONAL PORTFOLIO

The portfolio becomes the primary progression system.
It replaces:
XP
Levels
Achievement hunting
Simple scores
Instead it documents:
Knowledge
Skills
Judgement
Leadership
Innovation
Entrepreneurship
Research capability
Sustainability
Reflection
Communication
Every entry must be evidence-based.
Example:
Leadership
Evidence:
Successfully resolved 14 staff conflicts.
Maintained staff satisfaction during recession.
Balanced economic and social sustainability.
This is evidence.
Not opinion.

## PROFESSIONAL MATURITY

Replace traditional levels.
Instead use:
Novice
↓
Practitioner
↓
Reflective Practitioner
↓
Professional
↓
Expert
The player advances through demonstrated competence.
Not accumulated experience points.

## KNOWLEDGE CAPITAL

Knowledge produces capital.
Examples:
Scientific knowledge
↓
New menu
↓
New customers
↓
Higher revenue
↓
Investment
↓
Business development
Knowledge is therefore a production resource.

## RESTAURANTS

Every restaurant is a living organisation.
Not merely a building.
Each restaurant possesses:
identity
history
culture
economy
staff
customers
reputation
suppliers
competencies
development potential
Restaurants evolve.

## ADAPTIVE BUILDINGS

Most buildings in Grythyttan are adaptive.
Today:
Bakery
Tomorrow:
Wine bar
Later:
Restaurant
Then:
Hotel
Food laboratory
Research kitchen
Innovation hub
Only historically fixed landmarks remain permanent.

## INVESTMENT

The player invests before knowing future events.
Investment creates preparedness.
Preparedness determines resilience.
This principle remains from previous prototypes.

## EVENTS

Events are no longer drawn from cards.
Events emerge from simulation.
Examples:
Poor hygiene
↓
Inspection
Excellent reputation
↓
Media attention
Excellent sustainability
↓
New grants
Weak finances
↓
Loan difficulties
The simulation generates consequences.

## SEASONS

Time matters.
Weather matters.
Tourism matters.
Harvest matters.
Academic calendar matters.
Every season changes the village.

## NPCs

NPCs live independent lives.
They
study
work
eat
travel
buy
learn
teach
retire
open businesses
close businesses
change jobs
They remember previous interactions.

## CAMPUS GRYTHYTTAN

Campus is not simply a university building.
It is the world's knowledge engine.
Players may
study
conduct research
participate in seminars
meet researchers
develop innovations
publish work
receive supervision
Knowledge originates here.

## THE LIBRARY

The library is a physical place.
Not a menu.
Players visit it.
Search literature.
Read research.
Compare sources.
Unlock understanding.

## THE GASTRONOMIC THEATRE

A unique learning arena.
Players rehearse
hospitality
service
ethics
communication
presentation
guest interaction
leadership
reflection

## KALASTORGET

The social centre.
Contains:
festivals
public debates
markets
food events
competitions
temporary restaurants
lectures
community interaction

## QUESTIONS

Questions remain.
But questions are no longer the game.
Questions support learning.
Examples:
Acquire scientific understanding.
Unlock new methods.
Improve judgement.
Questions unlock capability.
Never points.

## QUESTION PROGRESSION

Questions progress through professional maturity.
Not difficulty alone.
Stage 1
Remember.
Stage 2
Understand.
Stage 3
Apply.
Stage 4
Analyse.
Stage 5
Evaluate.
Stage 6
Create.
The highest stages primarily assess Phronesis.

## SIMULATION FIRST

The simulation creates problems.
Knowledge solves them.
Not the opposite.

## SUSTAINABILITY

Economic.
Social.
Ecological.
Cultural.
Scientific.
These capitals constantly interact.
No optimal strategy exists.
Only trade-offs.

## RESEARCH

The game itself becomes a research platform.
With participant consent:
anonymous competence development
decision patterns
learning trajectories
professional judgement
can be studied scientifically.

## EDUCATIONAL USE

The portfolio supports:
Before university
Diagnostic competence profile.
During education
Progress tracking.
After graduation
Professional competence portfolio.
The portfolio is **not** designed as a formal admissions test.
However, it may serve as a validated diagnostic instrument and learning support tool.

## DESIGN PRINCIPLES

Never reward memorisation alone.
Reward understanding.
Reward application.
Reward judgement.
Reward reflection.
Reward ethical leadership.
Reward sustainable decisions.
Reward long-term thinking.
Reward curiosity.
Reward collaboration.

## DEFINITION OF SUCCESS

A successful player has not merely built a profitable restaurant.
A successful player has developed professional competence.
The village becomes stronger.
The organisations become stronger.
The player becomes stronger.
The portfolio documents that journey.

## FINAL PRINCIPLE

**Nexus Studio is not about winning.**
It is about becoming the kind of professional who is capable of improving the real world.
