# THE ORIGIN
**Version:** 0.1  
**Status:** Draft  
**Work Package:** WP-02

---

# Purpose

The Origin defines the emotional beginning of Nexus.

The player's journey does not begin by pressing "Start".

It begins by arriving.

---

# Design Goal

The first ten minutes must create:

- curiosity
- calm
- anticipation
- respect
- belonging

The player should feel:

> "I have arrived somewhere that existed before me and will continue after me."

---

# Setting

Location: Grythyttan, Sweden

Season:
The default opening takes place during a warm July morning when admission assessments are about to begin.

Time:
08:14

Weather:
Clear sky after light morning rain.

---

# Opening Sequence

The player is seated on a regional bus travelling through the forests of Bergslagen.

No tutorial appears.

The player can:

- look through the window
- read an admission folder
- observe other applicants
- remain silent

After a final bend the bus enters Grythyttan.

The first landmark is the Sevilla Pavilion.

No dramatic music.

Only birds, wind and gravel beneath the arriving passengers.

---

# First Text

"Everyone arrives with dreams.

No one yet knows who they will become."

The text fades.

Control is given to the player.

---

# Design Principles

1. The world is older than the player.
2. Atmosphere precedes mechanics.
3. Curiosity precedes reward.
4. Identity is discovered, not selected.
5. Grythyttan is both a real place and the symbolic origin of the Nexus world.

---

# MVP Scope

Included:

- Arrival
- Bus stop
- Sevilla Pavilion
- First path to campus
- Ambient sound
- Intro dialogue

Excluded:

- Economy
- Multiplayer
- Business ownership
- Quizzes
- Banking

These systems begin after the initiation.

---

# Acceptance Criteria

A first-time player should:

- remember arriving,
- recognise Grythyttan,
- understand that knowledge matters,
- want to continue walking.
