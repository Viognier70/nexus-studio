# The Initiation

**Version:** 0.1  
**Status:** Draft  
**Owner:** Game Design

## Purpose

The initiation identifies starting strengths, curiosities and possible pathways without fixing the player in a permanent role.

## Inputs

- optional real-world background,
- stated dreams and curiosities,
- short knowledge questions,
- practical micro-scenarios,
- judgement situations,
- interaction preferences.

## Assessment principle

The initiation does not answer “What are you?”

It helps answer:

- What do you already understand?
- What can you currently do?
- What situations interest you?
- Where might your hidden competence matter?
- What could you explore next?

## Outputs

The player receives:

- a multidimensional starting profile,
- three suggested opportunities,
- one unexpected competence hypothesis,
- access to all foundational learning,
- no permanent role lock.

## Example

A nurse who dreams of opening a bakery may show strengths in hygiene, risk awareness, stress management and care. These strengths can improve early employment options while baking competence is developed.

## Fairness rules

- No paid shortcut to certification.
- No exclusion based on occupation, age or prior formal education.
- Experts receive appropriately difficult assessments.
- Newcomers receive credible learning paths.
- The system must distinguish verified evidence from self-reported experience.

## Privacy

Real-world profile information is optional and private by default.
