Establish the narrative and experiential foundation of The Origin.

## Claude Code instruction

Review all files in `documentation/world/`.

Do not rewrite them.

Produce a review report identifying:

1. contradictions,
2. undefined player interactions,
3. missing state transitions,
4. content requiring legal or factual verification,
5. mobile/desktop conflicts,
6. implementation dependencies.

Do not initialise an application or create production code.

The Vision Owner must verify:

- memories and traditions,
- representation of Grythyttan,
- terminology,
- authenticity,
- whether the emotional tone is correct.

- the first-hour journey is coherent,
- the real-place rights checkpoint is recorded,
- the initiation does not lock roles,
- at least one gameplay consequence exists,
