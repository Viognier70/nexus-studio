# Sevilla Pavilion

**Version:** 0.1  
**Status:** Draft  
**Owner:** World and Art Direction

## Narrative role

The Sevilla Pavilion is the player's first visual symbol of Nexus: an international cultural object appearing unexpectedly within the Bergslagen landscape.

It represents:

- Scandinavian design,
- cultural identity,
- innovation,
- hospitality,
- confidence in the future.

## Use in the opening

The pavilion is revealed gradually rather than through a dramatic cutscene. The player should first notice its geometry through trees and then understand its scale and detail while approaching.

## Gameplay functions

In the first prototype it serves as:

- registration location,
- initiation gathering point,
- temporary exhibition space,
- public announcement site.

Later it may host:

- design competitions,
- research exhibitions,
- investor presentations,
- temporary restaurants,
- annual Origin events.

## Art constraints

- Avoid fantasy exaggeration.
- Preserve material specificity.
- Use natural light and landscape contrast.
- The building should feel exceptional because of context, not because it glows or behaves magically.

## Rights checkpoint

Before production use, verify permissions concerning architectural depiction, naming and commercial representation.
