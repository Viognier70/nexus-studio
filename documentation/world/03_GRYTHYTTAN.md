# Grythyttan

**Version:** 0.1  
**Status:** Draft  
**Owner:** World Design

## World function

Grythyttan is the first persistent settlement in Nexus and the shared origin of all early players.

It is not a backdrop. It is a social, educational and economic system with:

- campus life,
- local residents,
- hospitality businesses,
- public institutions,
- traditions,
- available and occupied premises,
- seasonal demand,
- local reputation.

## Design identity

Grythyttan should feel:

- geographically small,
- culturally dense,
- surrounded by forest and water,
- simultaneously traditional and experimental,
- recognisable to people who know the real village,
- understandable to players who have never visited Sweden.

## Spatial layers

### Campus zone

Education, assessment, research, ceremonies and student social life.

### Village centre

Shops, cafés, services, public noticeboards and social encounters.

### Hospitality corridor

Existing and player-created gastronomic businesses.

### Landscape edge

Forest, lake, trails, producers and seasonal resources.

### Expansion plots

A limited number of development sites activated as population and demand grow.

## Persistence

The village changes through:

- player businesses,
- closures and bankruptcies,
- festivals,
- seasonal visual changes,
- infrastructure scenarios,
- local reputation,
- collective sustainability outcomes.

## Authenticity rule

Real geography and recognisable landmarks may guide the map, but businesses, characters and future development must be fictionalised unless rights and permissions are secured.
