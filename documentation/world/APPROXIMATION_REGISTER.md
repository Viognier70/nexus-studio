- 2026-07-23 — **ORDER 004 / ORDER 005 PASS 1** executed for the eleven District 1 landmarks. Root-cause coordinate-frame bug identified from the m3-district capture: the extrusion pipeline placed each landmark's walls at `world Z = -poly.y` while overlaid decor was placed at `world Z = +poly.y`, opening a 2 × centre[1] gap that scaled with each landmark's distance from the OSM projection centre (~35 m for the church, ~78 m for Gästgivaregård, ~170 m for Måltidens hus, ~220 m for Herrgården). The fix, first proven on the church rev 2 and now applied to every crafted District 1 landmark, is a shared `useLandmarkWallGeo` helper that negates the shape Y before extrusion, centres the shape at the polygon centroid, and lives inside a single `<group>` anchored at `landmark.position`. Walls and decor share one local frame and cannot come apart. `THREE.DoubleSide` on the wall material defends against CW-wound OSM polygons. In the same pass, all invented silhouette detail on Måltidens hus (Sevillapaviljongen model), Gästgivaregården (already stripped), Pizzans Hus (hipped roof, chimney, sign), Herrgården (mansard stack), the Old Railway Station (long gable, ridge) is removed. Cornelis, Glass & Choklad, Antikvariatet CraftedNode boxes are replaced by `ApproximationMarker`. Torget PASS 1 report claimed the OSM polygon became the ground shape — that was incorrect, way `w122157681` is stored under `roads` and is a linear feature not an area polygon, so the render still uses a 36 × 30 placeholder. Skola and IP are outside the ORDER 004 list and retain their prior (still-offset) state — they will be brought into the new frame when their district enters reconstruction. Living-world systems, OSM roads / water / forest / terrain, traffic, pedestrians, gatherers, chimney smoke, commercial status tints and camera are all untouched.

- 2026-08-08 — **ORDER 042 §3.2 PlayerBusiness "brown film" — never a rendering bug; two fixes made on diagnosis without measurement; both stay because they are correct in substance.** Vision Owner reported a translucent brown tint at close zoom during §3.2 review; the tint had been the top face of the wall's `ExtrudeGeometry` cap plus the roof cap plus the walls themselves, all rendering mid-fade at the developer-preset distance where every fade curve happens to sit at 50 %. Three rounds of investigation, none of which measured:

   1. **depthWrite fix (commit `e896032`).** Diagnosed as "transparent walls with `depthWrite:true` cull other transparents". Applied `mat.depthWrite = wallOpacity > 0.5` to walls, matching the roof pattern. This IS the correct discipline for transparent geometry (missing depthWrite control causes distinct sort artifacts across the transparent bucket) but it did not address the brown film, because the film was not a sort artifact.

   2. **Side-quad wall geometry (commit `123757e`).** Diagnosed as "ExtrudeGeometry cap tints from above". Replaced the wall `ExtrudeGeometry` with a hand-built `BufferGeometry` of just the side quads, no top or bottom cap. This IS the correct geometry (a wall should be a wall, not a solid prism) but it also did not fix the film, because the ROOF cap was still an `ExtrudeGeometry` mid-fade AND the side walls themselves were still 50 % translucent from every angle under `DoubleSide`.

   3. **Wall fade to 0 (commit `d9b0ba8`).** Only after the developer-preset was moved inside the interior threshold (so the roof cap became invisible) and the Vision Owner reported the residual tint being "walls seen from outside — DoubleSide on ~50 % alpha tints everything behind the nearest wall", was the actual mechanism identified: any translucent surface with `α > 0` blends its colour into whatever is behind it, no matter how depthWrite / winding / side is configured.    **Pattern-note (not the sixth-occurrence pattern above; a different one).** Debugging by naming plausible mechanisms — sort order, depthWrite, side, extrude cap — and applying the fix for each in turn without measuring what is actually on the screen. In this case the second- and third-order fixes both proved correct in their own right, so the code ended up in a better place than it started; but that outcome was luck, not method. **The right method was the third instruction: log the actual values, or better, name the surface being rendered from direct observation.** The Vision Owner's identification "det är väggarna sedda utifrån" cut through three rounds of speculation in one sentence.

   The two fixes stay: `depthWrite` toggling on transparent materials is correct discipline regardless, and side-only wall geometry is the right shape for a wall. The diagnostic log was TEMPORARY and was removed in the same commit that landed the actual fix.
